## 🏛️ About Techseum

**Techseum** is a modern digital technology museum created to bridge the gap between abstract engineering concepts and visual intuition. Instead of static text and complex mathematical equations, Techseum transforms how hardware and digital protocols work into **continuous 60 FPS state machine animations, interactive Three.js 3D spatial models, virtual physics workbenches, and an intelligent AI docent.**

---

## ✨ Key Highlights

- **🌐 Dual 2D & 3D Simulation Engine**:
  - Continuous 60 FPS SVG diagrams visualizing physical and digital data flows.
  - Interactive Three.js 3D models with 360° orbital controls, exploded component views, and real-time screen callout pins.

- **🤖 Ask AI Guide**:
  - Integrated AI museum docent powered by Google Gemini, capable of answering deep-dive questions and contextual technical inquiries for any exhibit.

- **🧪 Virtual Physics Laboratories**:
  - Hands-on interactive workbenches for Ohm's Law circuit dynamics, camera optics ray-tracing, and network packet MTU fragmentation.

- **⚡ "How Web Works" Scenario**:
  - A microsecond-by-microsecond interactive journey exploring what happens behind the scenes when you type `google.com` and press Enter.

- **🏆 Gamified Learning & Community**:
  - Skill verification challenges, XP rewards, architectural achievement badges, and a real-time global leaderboard.

- **🌍 Bilingual Localization & Newsletter**:
  - Full seamless support for both **Bahasa Indonesia** and **English**, along with an automated weekly exhibit newsletter system.

## 🔬 Exhibition Categories

Techseum organizes its interactive collection across 5 primary domains:

1. **💻 Computing & Silicon**: Microprocessors, GPU parallelism, DRAM cells, NAND flash, lithography, and quantum computing.
2. **📡 Networking & RF**: Wi-Fi modulation, 5G beamforming, DNS resolution, Bluetooth, and undersea fiber optics.
3. **⚡ Electronics & Hardware**: CMOS image sensors, battery chemistry, MEMS sensors, and multi-layer PCBs.
4. **📱 Everyday & Mobile Tech**: Capacitive touchscreens, GPS trilateration, Active Noise Cancellation (ANC), OLED displays, and biometric sensors.
5. **🧠 Modern Intelligent Tech**: Artificial neural networks and deep learning forward propagation.
